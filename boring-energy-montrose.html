Technical Concept

Location & Head

  • • Uncompahgre Plateau to valley floor offers significant natural elevation relief
  • • Precedent: Ridgway Dam ~200 ft head, 7–8 MW; South Canal ~7.5 MW
  • • Target alignment for 250–500+ ft effective head over ~1 mile

Power & Storage

  • • Target range 2–8 MW continuous / dispatchable
  • • Pumped hydro mode pairs with Colorado solar/wind growth
  • • Underground cavern for AI/BTC compute with natural cooling (PUE < 1.1)

AWH Integration

Modular fog nets and biomimetic collectors on the plateau capture additional moisture. Water routes into the hydro storage loop or dedicated agricultural/drought buffer storage — increasing system resilience without new surface diversions.
