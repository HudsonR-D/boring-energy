AWH SERIES

White Papers

Two companion papers establishing the hydrologic and commercial case for monitored commercial-scale atmospheric water harvesting under Colorado’s no-injury framework.

PAPER 1 Policy & Hydrologic Synthesis

Negligible Hydrological Impact of Atmospheric Water Harvesting at Commercial Scales

A synthesis of peer-reviewed studies, Colorado precipitation pilot data, and atmospheric moisture dynamics. Concludes that commercial-scale AWH should be treated as a negligible-impact practice under the same “no injury” lens already applied to precipitation harvesting.

• Atmospheric moisture residence time ~8 days; weak local recycling
• Front Range moisture largely advective (Pacific + western U.S.)
• Global stress test: even humanitarian-scale AWH is 0.01% of land ET
• Recommends pilot extension or negligible-impact exemption before July 2026 sunset
Version 2.0 • May 2026 PDF available
PAPER 2 Commercial Opportunity

Quantified Opportunity for Commercial Atmospheric Water Harvesting in Colorado

Companion paper quantifying scale, net hydrologic impact, and senior-rights protection under a monitored regulatory pathway. Focuses on industrial users with concentrated, metered demand (lumber, CEA, data centers, manufacturing).

• Montrose Forest Products case: ~107 AF/yr process demand
• Conservative scenarios: 1,000–7,500 AF/yr offset potential
• Full metering + State Engineer curtailment retained
• Aligns with Colorado Water Plan decentralized supply goals
Version 2.1 • June 2026 Markdown / PDF
