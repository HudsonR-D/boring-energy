Core Idea

Retrofit the existing subsurface flame trench, deluge manifold, sump pumps, and retention ponds with sealed high-temperature ducting, heat-recovery steam generators (HRSG) or ORC turbines, and condensers. Route the plume/steam for condensation (harvesting both combustion and deluge water) and electricity generation.

Fail-safe design: Instant bypass reverts to today’s open-plume mode on any anomaly. All hardware is ground-side — zero impact on vehicle performance or reusability.
